Need a hand? Check the answers below, or write to us. We read every message.

How do I add a task fast?

Tap + and type like you talk: “Call Mom tomorrow 6pm !!”. Sova reads the day, the time, repeats like “every Monday”, and !! or !!! for priority.

Why didn’t I get a task alert?

Alerts only fire for tasks with a time. Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Sova, and the alert timing in Sova’s Settings.

How do repeating tasks work?

When you finish a repeating task, Sova creates the next one automatically. Tap Undo if you didn’t mean to.

How do I add the widgets?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, then search for Sova. The Rituals widget lets you tick rituals right from the Home Screen.

What does Sova Plus include?

Unlimited rituals and goals, iCloud sync across your devices and extra app icons. Free includes unlimited tasks, 3 rituals and 1 goal.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

Restore: Sova → Settings → Restore purchases. Cancel: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Sova.

How does iCloud sync work?

With Plus, turn on Settings → Sync with iCloud, then reopen Sova. Your data syncs privately through your own iCloud account.
