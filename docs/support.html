Using Sova

Add a task fast

Tap + and type the way you'd say it: “Call Mom tomorrow 6pm !!”. Sova reads the day, the time, repeats like “every Monday” and priority from !! or !!!.

Using Sova

Repeating tasks

Pick daily, weekdays, weekly, monthly or yearly when you edit a task. Finishing one creates the next. Tap Undo if you didn't mean to.

Using Sova

Add the widgets

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for Sova. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold it, tap Customize, then the widget area.

Using Sova

Alerts don't arrive

Alerts only fire for tasks with a time. Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Sova, and the alert timing in Sova → Settings → Calendar & alerts.

Sova Plus

iCloud sync

With Plus, turn on Sova → Settings → Sync with iCloud, then reopen Sova. Make sure you're signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on each device.

Sova Plus

Restore purchases

On a new iPhone, open Sova → Settings → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought Plus with.

Sova Plus

Cancel a subscription

Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Sova. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for, and nothing is deleted.

Sova Plus

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

Delete your data

Sova → Settings → Erase All Data removes everything. Deleting the app also removes it from this iPhone. With iCloud sync on, also check iCloud settings.
