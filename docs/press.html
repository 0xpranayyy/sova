About Sova

Short: Sova is a friendly iPhone app for tasks, daily habits and big goals, with a little owl cheering you on.

Longer: Sova keeps tasks, rituals and goals in one place. Type a task the way you'd say it and Sova fills in the date, time and priority. Rituals track streaks on the days you choose, goals break into steps, and widgets let you tick things off from the Home Screen. It's free, with Sova Plus for unlimited rituals and goals, iCloud sync and app icons. There's no account and no tracking.
