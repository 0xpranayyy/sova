Questions

Quick answers about using Sova, Plus and your data.

Still stuck?The support page has more answers and a way to reach us.
How do I add a task fast?

Tap + and type the way you would say it, like “Call Mom tomorrow 6pm !!”. Sova reads the day, the time, repeats like “every Monday” and priority from !! or !!!.

How do repeating tasks work?

Pick daily, weekdays, weekly, monthly or yearly. When you finish one, Sova creates the next one automatically. Tap Undo if you didn't mean to.

How do I add the widgets?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget and search for Sova. The Rituals widget lets you tick habits off without opening the app.

Does Sova work offline?

Yes. Everything works without a connection. With Sova Plus and iCloud sync on, changes sync the next time you're online.

Is Sova free?

Yes. Unlimited tasks, 5 rituals, 3 active goals, widgets, Siri, Insights and dark mode are free with no time limit.

What happens if I cancel Plus?

You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for. After that, nothing is deleted. You just can't add more than 5 rituals or 3 active goals.

How do I cancel or restore?

Cancel in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Sova. To restore on a new iPhone, open Sova → Settings → Restore purchases.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up, email or password. Open the app and start.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud sync with Plus, it's kept in your own private iCloud. We never see your data, and there is no tracking or ads.
