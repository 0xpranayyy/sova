Questions? Hoo‑hoo.

Is Sova free?

Yes. Unlimited tasks, 3 rituals, 1 goal, widgets, Siri and dark mode are free forever. Sova Plus unlocks unlimited rituals and goals, iCloud sync and extra app icons.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start. There's no sign-up, no email and no password.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. With Sova Plus you can turn on iCloud sync, which keeps it in your own private iCloud. We never see your data.

What can quick capture understand?

Days and times (“tomorrow 6pm”, “Friday”), repeats (“every Monday”, “daily”) and priority (“!!” or “!!!”). Try “Water plants every Sunday 9am”.

How do I cancel Sova Plus?

Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Sova. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

Is there an iPad or Mac version?

Sova 1.0 is made for iPhone. More is on the owl's wish list.
