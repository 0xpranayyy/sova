Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Short version: Sova doesn't collect any of your data. No account, no tracking, no ads, no analytics.

What you add stays yours

Your tasks, rituals, goals, name and settings are stored on your device. We never see them, and they're never sent to us or to anyone else.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on iCloud sync with Sova Plus, your data is stored in your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so it can appear on your other devices. Only you can access it. Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud.

Notifications

Reminders and task alerts are scheduled on your device by iOS. Nothing about them leaves your device.

Purchases

Sova Plus is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never receive your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether Plus is active.

Siri, Shortcuts and widgets

When you use Sova with Siri, Shortcuts or widgets, everything is processed on your device by the app itself.

Children

Sova doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Erase All Data in the app, or delete the app. If iCloud sync is on, you can also manage Sova's data in your iPhone's iCloud settings.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions? Use "Help & feedback" in Sova's Settings.
